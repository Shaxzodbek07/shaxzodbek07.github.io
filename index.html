Silk Road Caravan

Bandits are waiting along the trade road. Your caravan only moves while you stay beside it, so every fight is a choice between pushing on and holding the line.

Play in your browser

Free. Works on PC and on phones held sideways.

How a journey goes

  1. Leave the city

    Walk beside the wagon and it rolls along the road. Walk away and it waits for you, alone and unprotected.

  2. Survive the ambushes

    Raiders run alongside the wagon to hack at it, blockers stand on the road to stop it, and archers aim from the dunes. A red line shows where each arrow will fly.

  3. Fight with combos and skills

    Chain a three-hit sword combo, dash through a line of bandits, or spin to throw back everyone crowding the wagon. Gold from every bandit pays for repairs.

  4. Cross the mountain pass

    A red circle on the ground means a boulder is coming. Time your push through the canyon, or lure the bandits under the rocks.

  5. Reach the gate

    Bring the caravan home in one piece to win the level.
