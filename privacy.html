Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Silk Road Caravan is a free browser game made by Shahzod as a student project. This page explains what information is used when you visit this site and play the game.

Information the game collects

The game does not ask for your name, email, or any other personal information, and it does not create accounts. Game settings may be stored in your own browser so they are remembered next time. This information stays on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Monetag, a third-party advertising network. Ads appear only between rounds or when you choose to watch one for a reward. Monetag may use cookies and similar technologies, and may collect information such as your device type, browser, approximate location, and IP address to show and measure ads. How Monetag handles this information is described in Monetag's own privacy policy on monetag.com.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can use your browser's privacy features to limit tracking. The game still works if you do.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the developer's GitHub profile: github.com/Shaxzodbek07.
